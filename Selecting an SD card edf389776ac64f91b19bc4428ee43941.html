Everyday Use

32GB or less SDHC cards work well and will be the most cost effective.

  • SDXC cards work as well but I've done less testing with these.

High Reliability

PyCubed's smallsat paper contains a good discussion on this.

Recently, I've been using SanDisk "High Endurance SLC" cards. They're a cost efficient SLC card that's easy to find from Mouser, Digi-Key, etc...

    SDSDQED-008G-XI
